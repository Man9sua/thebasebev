A café milkshake that sells in 2027 needs one consistent base and one or two trend accents, not a new recipe for every flavour. The standard build is **30 g of milkshake base, 200 ml milk and 100 g ice, blended for 30 seconds into a 12 oz cup**, and every recipe below starts from it. Change the fruit, keep the base, and the drink tastes the same on every shift.

## What milkshake flavours are trending for 2027?

The 2027 forecasts point in a few clear directions that fit a milkshake menu:

1. **Dark and dramatic fruits.** Deep red and purple fruits are moving from seasonal use to year round premium drivers, according to the [Imbibe 2027 Flavor Forecast](https://www.preparedfoods.com/articles/131677-imbibe-forecasts-seven-flavor-territories-shaping-beverage-innovation).
2. **Sweet meets savoury.** Sweet, salt and acid combinations are one of the flavour territories shaping beverage innovation, as reported by [FoodNavigator USA](https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2026/07/06/what-are-the-7-flavour-trends-set-to-impact-us-beverages-in-2027/).
3. **Pistachio.** In the UAE, pistachio has moved from a dessert flavour to one of the most ordered drink flavours on café menus, as [Curly Tales](https://curlytales.com/middle-east/food/rutab-drinks-to-hojicha-lattes-viral-sips-taking-over-the-uae-right-now/) notes.
4. **Floral notes.** Lavender and similar aromatics add perceived value without making the drink sweeter.

Each of the four builds below covers one of these directions.

## How do you make a café milkshake with a powder base?

The rule from our barista standard: **base, liquid, ice, flavour**. Measure every component, blend 30 seconds, and leave 10 to 20 ml headroom in the cup. Use no more than two flavour accents in one drink.

### 1. Pearfect Pistachio (pistachio trend)

1. Vanilla milkshake base: 30 g
2. Milk: 200 ml
3. Pistachio sauce: 35 g in the blend
4. Pear, fresh or frozen: 30 g
5. Ice: 100 g

Blend all ingredients until smooth and pour into a 12 oz glass. Finish with a 10 g drizzle of pistachio sauce and 1 g of pistachio crumbs.

### 2. Blueberry Acai (dark fruit trend)

1. Yogurt milkshake base: 30 g
2. Milk: 200 ml
3. Frozen acai purée: 50 g
4. Fresh blueberries: 5 g
5. Ice: 100 g

Blend until smooth and pour into a 12 oz glass. Dust the top with a freeze dried blueberry garnish and add one whole blueberry.

### 3. Apple Feta Creamshake (sweet savoury trend)

1. Yogurt milkshake base: 30 g
2. Milk: 200 ml
3. Green apple: 40 g
4. Feta cheese: 10 g
5. Lemon juice: 10 g
6. Ice: 100 g

Blend until smooth and pour into a 12 oz glass. Garnish with a mint leaf or an apple slice. The feta adds salt and body, the lemon keeps it fresh, and the yogurt base ties both together.

### 4. Lavender Lychee (floral trend)

1. Yogurt milkshake base: 30 g
2. Milk: 200 ml
3. Fresh lychee: 40 g
4. Dried lavender: 2 g
5. Ice: 100 g

Blend until smooth and pour into a 12 oz glass. Garnish with a fresh lychee and a few lavender petals. Keep the lavender at 2 g; more turns the drink soapy.

## Why use one base for the whole milkshake menu?

1. **Consistency.** A new barista follows the same 30 g, 200 ml, 100 g build for every shake. Only the accent changes.
2. **Less stock.** One or two bases replace a shelf of flavoured syrups and ice cream tubs.
3. **Faster service.** Blending takes 30 seconds, with no scooping or thawing.
4. **Easy rotation.** You can swap the trend accent each season without retraining the team.

## FAQ

### How much milkshake powder do I need per drink?

Use 30 g of base with 200 ml milk and 100 g ice for a 12 oz cup. Blend for 30 seconds.

### Can I make these milkshakes with plant milk?

Yes. THE BASE milkshake bases work with plant milk. Keep the same 200 ml volume so the texture stays consistent.

### Do I need ice cream for a café milkshake?

No. The base and 100 g of ice give the body. That keeps the build faster and the cost per cup easier to control.

### How many flavours should a milkshake menu have?

Start with one classic plus three or four trend builds like the ones above, all from the same base. Rotate the trend builds each season.
