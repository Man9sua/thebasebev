Iced karak chai is the cold version of the spiced, milky karak tea sold across the UAE. A café can make it with a single shake from a dry karak base: 30 g of base and 200 ml milk, dry shaken and poured over 100 g ice in a 12 oz cup. Below are four signature builds with exact doses, from a fruit led peach karak to a karak mocha.

## Why do cafés in the UAE need an iced karak?

Karak is the everyday tea of the UAE, sold at every price point from roadside cafeterias to specialty cafés, as [Gulf News' guide to karak spots](https://gulfnews.com/things-to-do/cafes-restaurants/explore-the-best-spots-in-dubai-to-enjoy-a-classic-cup-of-karak-chai-1.500135322) shows. Cold versions followed the heat: [Khaleej Times reported](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/food/beat-dubais-heat-with-these-iced-karaks) eateries across Dubai adding iced karak in cardamom, ginger, masala and saffron flavours.

For a café, an iced karak list does three jobs:

- **A local drink for a cold menu.** Guests already know karak, so a signature version needs little explanation at the counter.
- **Year round sales.** Iced drinks sell through most of the Gulf year, and the same base runs hot in winter evenings.
- **One base, several drinks.** All four builds below start from the same karak powder and the same shake.

## What is the standard for an iced karak?

All four builds share one frame:

1. Cup: **12 oz**.
2. Base: **25 to 30 g** karak chai base.
3. Milk: **150 to 200 ml**.
4. Ice: **100 g** in the cup.
5. Method: **dry shake** base, milk and accents without ice until fully mixed and slightly frothy, then pour over ice.

Dry shaking dissolves the powder completely and builds a light foam. Put the ice in the cup, not the shaker, so the drink is not diluted before it reaches the guest. For a classic hot karak, the standard is 25 g base with 150 ml milk or water in an 8 oz cup.

## How do you make a Peach Breeze karak?

A fruit forward karak with a herbal finish.

- 30 g karak base
- 200 ml milk
- 30 g peach purée
- 20 g rosemary syrup
- 100 g ice

1. Combine the milk, karak base, peach purée and rosemary syrup in a shaker.
2. Dry shake until fully mixed and slightly frothy.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with a rosemary sprig or a peach slice.

## How do you make an Orange Chai?

Citrus and clove, close to the spice profile of a winter karak but served cold.

- 30 g karak base
- 200 ml milk
- 30 ml fresh orange juice
- 20 ml clove syrup
- 100 g ice

1. Combine all ingredients except the ice in a shaker.
2. Dry shake until fully mixed and slightly frothy.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with a dehydrated orange slice and a whole clove.

## How do you make a Mellow Spice karak?

The simplest of the four: karak with ginger warmth.

- 30 g karak base
- 200 ml milk
- 20 ml lemongrass ginger syrup
- 100 g ice

1. Dry shake the karak base with the milk until well combined.
2. Add the syrup and shake again.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with candied ginger.

## How do you make a Karak Mocha?

Karak, chocolate and a double ristretto, for guests who want caffeine and spice in one cup.

- 25 g karak base
- 10 g classic [chocolate base](/chocolate)
- 30 ml espresso (double ristretto)
- 150 ml milk
- 100 g ice
- 50 g tapioca pearls (optional)
- Cinnamon stick, star anise and a dash of cinnamon powder for garnish

1. Brew the espresso and set it aside.
2. Dry shake the milk, karak base and chocolate base until fully mixed and slightly frothy.
3. Add ice, and tapioca pearls if used, to a 12 oz glass.
4. Pour the karak mixture and the espresso over the ice.
5. Garnish with the cinnamon stick, star anise and cinnamon powder.

## FAQ

### How much karak base goes into an iced karak?

These builds use 25 to 30 g of karak base with 150 to 200 ml milk and 100 g ice in a 12 oz cup.

### Should iced karak be shaken with ice?

No. Dry shake the base and milk first, then pour over ice in the cup. Shaking with ice dilutes the drink before it is served.

### Can the same base be used for hot karak?

Yes. For a hot 8 oz karak, use 25 g base with 150 ml milk or water, steamed or stirred.

### Can iced karak be made with plant milk?

Yes. The bases work with plant milk, so oat or almond milk can replace dairy at the same volume. Test citrus builds with your plant milk before launch.
