Saffron hot chocolate is a chocolate drink spiced with the saffron profile of Gulf karak tea. A café can build it from two dry bases, a chocolate base and a saffron karak chai latte base, steamed together with milk on the espresso machine wand. Below are four winter builds for an 8 oz cup with exact doses.

## Why is saffron hot chocolate showing up on Gulf winter menus?

Karak is the everyday tea of the UAE, and saffron karak is a standard item on karak menus across Dubai, as [local café guides show](https://gulfnews.com/things-to-do/cafes-restaurants/explore-the-best-spots-in-dubai-to-enjoy-a-classic-cup-of-karak-chai-1.500135322). Pairing that flavour with chocolate is getting attention: an Emirati hot chocolate spiced with [saffron and cardamom drew crowds at Davos](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/watch-emirati-hot-chocolate-served-in-snow-wins-hearts-in-switzerland?amp=1).

For a café this means:

- **A local signature** that guests recognise from karak but have not seen in a chocolate cup.
- **No espresso needed.** None of the four builds below contains coffee, which suits evening and family traffic.
- **No new equipment.** Every build is steamed on the espresso machine wand.

## What is the cup standard for these winter drinks?

All four builds share one frame:

1. Cup: **8 oz** (237 ml).
2. Target fill: **227 ml**, leaving **10 ml** headroom.
3. Milk: start with **180 ml** and adjust so the foam keeps the 10 ml headroom.
4. Steam to **60 to 65°C**.

Combine the bases and milk in the pitcher before steaming. Steaming dissolves the powder fully and you skip a separate mixing step.

## How do you make a Spiced Saffron Hot Choco?

The signature version, with a saffron cold foam on top.

- 20 g classic [chocolate base](/chocolate)
- 20 g saffron karak [chai latte base](/chai-latte)
- 180 ml milk
- 20 g saffron cold foam
- Cinnamon powder for dusting

1. Combine both bases and the milk in a steaming pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.
4. Top with 20 g saffron cold foam.
5. Finish with a dusting of cinnamon powder.

## Is there a simpler version without cold foam?

Yes. The **Spiced Saffron Hot Chocolate** uses the same 20 g chocolate base, 20 g saffron karak chai latte base and 180 ml milk, steamed to 60 to 65°C and finished with cinnamon. It skips the foam, so it is faster at peak hours and works well as the standard menu item, with the foam version as the upgrade.

## How do you make a Winter Saffron Date Masala Chai?

A chai led drink with date sweetness, close to what Gulf guests drink at home in winter.

- 25 g saffron karak chai latte base
- 180 ml milk
- 10 ml date syrup

1. Combine the chai base, date syrup and milk in a pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 60 to 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.

Our bases are medium sweet, so taste once with the 10 ml of date syrup before adding more.

## How do you make an Orange Blossom Hot Chocolate?

A lighter chocolate with a citrus finish, built from a chocolate base and a [Raf base](/raf-coffee). Raf bases contain no coffee; in this drink no espresso is added.

- 15 g classic chocolate base
- 15 g orange sundae Raf base
- 180 ml milk

1. Combine both bases and the milk in a pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 60 to 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.

## Can these drinks be made with plant milk or served iced?

Our bases work with plant milk, so oat or almond milk can replace dairy at the same 180 ml. They can also be served iced: dissolve the bases fully before building over ice, so no powder settles at the bottom of the cup.

## FAQ

### What does saffron hot chocolate taste like?

Chocolate first, then the warm floral note of saffron and the spice of karak. A dusting of cinnamon on top rounds it out.

### Does saffron hot chocolate contain caffeine?

The four builds here contain no coffee. The chai latte base is a tea style base, so check the product specification if you need a fully caffeine free claim on your menu.

### How many grams of base go into an 8 oz hot chocolate?

The builds above use 25 to 40 g of base in total with 180 ml milk: 20 g plus 20 g for the saffron chocolates, 15 g plus 15 g for the orange blossom version, and 25 g for the date masala chai.
