Winter in the Gulf is short and mild, but it changes what guests order. From November to February the evenings cool down, terraces fill up, and hot drinks start to outsell iced ones after sunset. A café that switches part of its menu for these months catches demand that an all-iced menu leaves on the table.

The trick is to add winter drinks that your team can make fast and the same way every time, without buying new equipment or training a new skill.

## Which hot drinks sell best in a Gulf winter?

These five are reliable because guests already know them and they all build on milk and a steam wand:

1. **Hot chocolate.** The best seller with families and non-coffee guests. Finish with marshmallows or a cocoa dusting for a higher price point.
2. **Chai latte and karak.** Spiced tea is a local habit, so it needs no explanation on the menu. Offer it both sweet and less sweet.
3. **Raf coffee.** Espresso with cream and vanilla, whipped together. Rich and dessert-like, it works as a signature drink.
4. **Spiced and seasonal lattes.** Cinnamon, cardamom, gingerbread or saffron on a latte base. One limited flavour per month keeps regulars curious.
5. **Ube or matcha latte, hot.** Colour still sells on social media in winter, and both work hot.

## How do you keep hot drinks consistent across shifts?

Hot drinks fail on consistency more than on recipe. One barista adds more syrup, another less chocolate, and the drink tastes different on every shift. A powdered base fixes this: the sweetness and flavour are set in the factory, and the barista only measures base and milk.

A typical build with a hot chocolate base:

- **35 g** chocolate base
- **200 ml** milk
- Stir in a pitcher until fully dissolved, steam or heat, pour into an 8 oz cup. About one minute from start to serve.

With a 500 g pouch you get 14 cups, which makes stock planning simple: count the cups you expect to sell, divide by 14, and you know how many pouches to order.

## What should you cut for winter?

Keep the menu short. For each winter drink you add, remove or pause one iced drink that slows down in the cooler months. Fewer items means faster service at the evening peak and less stock sitting open.

## How to price winter drinks

Work from cost per cup, not from the competitor's board. Add base, milk, cup, lid and any topping, then set the menu price so ingredients stay at the share of price you target for the rest of your menu. Toppings such as marshmallows, whipped cream or a chocolate drizzle cost little and let you charge more for the same drink.

## FAQ

### When should a UAE café switch to a winter menu?

Most cafés add hot specials in November and run them until late February or March, when daytime heat returns.

### Can the same bases be used for iced drinks?

Yes. Milk-based bases dissolve in cold milk as well, so one stock covers hot drinks in winter and iced drinks in summer.

### Do I need new equipment for these drinks?

No. A steam wand, a pitcher and a scale are enough for every drink above.
