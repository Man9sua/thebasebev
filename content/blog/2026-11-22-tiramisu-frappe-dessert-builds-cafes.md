A tiramisu frappe is a blended iced coffee that tastes like the dessert: espresso, a creamy cheese layer and cocoa. A café can make it from a dry vanilla frappe base in one blender cycle: 25 g base, 150 ml milk, 30 ml espresso, 30 g cream cheese or mascarpone, 4 g cocoa powder and 120 g ice in a 12 oz cup. Three more dessert builds follow.

## What is the standard for a dessert frappe?

Our everyday frappe standard is 35 g base, 200 ml milk and 150 g ice in a 14 oz cup, blended for 30 seconds. Dessert frappes use a lighter base dose in a **12 oz** cup, because the cream cheese, sauce or condensed milk adds body and sweetness of its own. Keep to the rule of **two flavour accents per drink** so the espresso still comes through.

## How do you make a Tiramisu Frappe?

- 25 g vanilla frappe base
- 150 ml milk
- 30 ml espresso
- 30 g cream cheese or mascarpone
- 4 g cocoa powder
- 120 g ice
- 1 ladyfinger biscuit, crushed, for garnish

1. Add the frappe base, milk, espresso, cream cheese, cocoa powder and ice to the blender.
2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
3. Pour into a 12 oz glass.
4. Finish with cream cheese [topping](/topping), a dusting of cocoa powder and the crushed ladyfinger.

## How do you make a Cream Crown Frappe?

A vanilla caramel frappe with ice cream in the blend and a cream cheese cap.

- 20 g vanilla frappe base
- 130 ml milk
- 30 ml espresso
- 1 scoop ice cream
- 10 g caramel sauce
- 10 g hazelnut or vanilla [sugar syrup](/sugar-syrup)
- 100 g ice
- 50 g cream cheese topping

1. Blend everything except the topping until smooth.
2. Pour into a 12 oz cup.
3. Top with 50 g cream cheese topping.
4. Drizzle caramel sauce and finish with crushed nuts.

## How do you make a Bonbon Frappe?

Espresso with condensed milk and chocolate.

- 25 g vanilla frappe base
- 30 ml espresso
- 40 g sweetened condensed milk
- 100 ml milk
- 10 g chocolate sauce
- 130 g ice
- 30 g cream cheese topping

1. Blend the frappe base, milk, espresso, condensed milk, chocolate sauce and ice until smooth and creamy.
2. Pour into a 12 oz glass.
3. Top with 30 g cream cheese topping and a dusting of cocoa powder or chocolate shavings.

Condensed milk is sweet, so taste the first cups before adding any syrup.

## Is there a dessert option without coffee?

Yes. The **Gianduja Cocoa** is a shaken iced chocolate with hazelnut, built on a [chocolate base](/chocolate) with no espresso.

- 45 g chocolate base
- 200 ml milk
- 15 ml hazelnut syrup
- 10 ml vanilla syrup
- A pinch of sea salt
- 100 g ice

1. Add the milk, chocolate base, both syrups and the sea salt to a shaker.
2. Dry shake well, without ice.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with crushed hazelnuts or chocolate shavings.

## FAQ

### What is in a tiramisu frappe?

Espresso, milk, a vanilla frappe base, cream cheese or mascarpone, cocoa powder and ice, blended and finished with cocoa and a crushed ladyfinger.

### How much frappe base goes into a 12 oz dessert frappe?

The builds above use 20 to 25 g of base. Our standard frappe without dessert accents uses 35 g base with 200 ml milk and 150 g ice in a 14 oz cup.

### Can these frappes be made with plant milk?

Yes. The bases work with plant milk at the same volume. Check the cream cheese topping and condensed milk separately if you need a fully dairy free drink.
