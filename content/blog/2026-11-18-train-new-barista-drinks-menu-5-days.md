A new barista can learn a full non-coffee drinks menu in five working days if you teach it by **preparation method**, not drink by drink, and every recipe uses a fixed dose in grams. Day 1 covers measuring, days 2 to 4 cover hot, iced and blended builds, and day 5 is a timed test on a real shift.

## Why does barista training need to be fast in the UAE?

Because you will repeat it often. [Gulf News reports](https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/uaes-fb-sector-has-work-to-do-on-employee-loyalty-1.2045946) that UAE hospitality and F&B have some of the highest staff turnover of any sector, and [industry figures for specialty coffee](https://roasters.app/business/why-your-barista-quit-turnover-patterns) put typical entry level barista tenure at 8 to 14 months. A short written plan with gram doses can be run by any shift lead, so quality does not leave with one senior barista.

## How should you structure the five days?

Group the menu into methods, one per day, so the trainee repeats the same hand movements:

- **Steam:** chai latte, chocolate, raf, matcha
- **Shake or stir:** iced tea, cordials, iced matcha, pure powders
- **Blend:** frappe and milkshake
- **Build in glass:** sparkling cordials, toppings, garnish

## Day 1: what should a new barista learn first?

Measuring, before any recipe.

1. **Weigh every dose in grams.** Scoops vary with how the powder settles; a scale does not.
2. Learn the bar formula: **base, liquid, ice, flavour = drink.**
3. Leave **10 to 20 ml headroom** in every cup.
4. Use a maximum of **two flavour accents** per drink.

## Day 2: how do you train hot milk drinks?

Standard doses for 8 oz hot drinks:

- **Chai Latte:** 25 g base, 150 ml milk or water, steam or stir
- **Chocolate:** 35 g base, 200 ml milk, steam
- **Matcha base:** 20 g, 200 ml milk, steam
- **Raf:** 20 g base, 200 ml milk, 20 ml espresso, steam
- **Pure powders (matcha, ube, hojicha, butterfly pea):** 3 g whisked into 40 ml water at 70°C, then 170 ml steamed milk

Our [Raf](/raf-coffee) and Cream Latte bases contain no coffee, so the espresso is always added fresh. Flavour options are on the [chai latte](/chai-latte) page. Drill: ten of each drink, weighing every dose.

## Day 3: how do you train iced and shaken drinks?

Standard doses for 12 oz cold drinks:

- **Iced Tea:** 20 g base, 150 ml water, 150 g ice, shake
- **Cordial:** 15 g base, 150 ml sparkling water, 150 g ice, build in glass
- **Iced Matcha base:** 30 g, 230 ml milk, 100 g ice, dry shake
- **Pure powders iced:** 4 g whisked into 40 ml water at 70°C, then 210 ml milk and 80 g ice
- **Topping or cold foam:** 50 g topping with 250 ml milk (ratio 1:5), frothed

Typical mistakes: skipping the dry shake on matcha and adding ice before the powder dissolves. More builds on the [iced tea](/iced-tea) page.

## Day 4: how do you train blended drinks and finishes?

- **Frappe:** 35 g base, 200 ml milk, 150 g ice, blend 30 seconds, 14 oz cup
- **Milkshake:** 30 g base, 200 ml milk, 100 g ice, blend 30 seconds, 12 oz cup
- **Sauces:** 15 to 20 g around the glass or on top
- **Fruit purée:** 60 to 70 g in the blender or at the bottom of the glass
- **Freeze dried garnish:** 1 to 2 g on top

Time the blender: under blended drinks are icy, over blended ones lose body. Recipes are on the [frappe](/frappe) page.

## Day 5: how do you sign off a new barista?

Run a test ticket of ten mixed drinks across all four methods and check:

1. Correct dose on the scale
2. Correct cup size and headroom
3. Taste against a reference cup from the head barista
4. Ticket time against your peak hour target

Sign off method by method. A trainee who passes steam and shake but not blend works hot and iced until blend is retested. After sign off, keep a gram recipe card at every station and weigh one finished drink per shift.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to train a barista on non-coffee drinks?

With a method based plan and gram doses, five working days is realistic. Espresso extraction and latte art take longer and are best trained separately.

### Should baristas use scoops or scales for powder premixes?

Scales. A scoop holds a different weight depending on how the powder settles, which changes sweetness and body.

### Can the same base be used for hot and iced drinks?

Yes. THE BASE bases can be steamed or served iced; only the dose, liquid and ice change.
