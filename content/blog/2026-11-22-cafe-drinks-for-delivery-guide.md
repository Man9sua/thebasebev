Café drinks for delivery work when you pick drinks that hold their texture through the ride, build them to a fixed dose, leave headroom in the cup and pack fragile elements separately. Shaken iced teas, stirred chai and steamed hot chocolate travel well. Blended frappes, sparkling drinks and foam crowns need changes before they go on a delivery menu.

## Why does delivery matter for café drink sales in the UAE?

Delivery is now a large share of the business. In the first half of 2026, [delivery made up 30.5% of restaurant orders in the UAE, up from 25.4% a year earlier](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uae-food-delivery-orders-rise-15-in-h1-as-diners-shift-away-from-eating-out), while dine-in orders fell. A drink that looks right on the bar can arrive melted, flat or spilled, and customers rate the café, not the rider.

## Which café drinks travel best for delivery?

Rank your menu by how much the drink changes in transit.

**Travel well**

- **Iced tea:** 20 g base, 150 ml water, 150 g ice, shaken, 12 oz cup. Dilution from melting ice is mild because the drink is already water based.
- **Chai latte:** 25 g base with 150 ml milk or water, steamed hot or stirred over ice, 8 oz cup. Spice flavour holds as it cools.
- **Hot chocolate:** 35 g base with 200 ml milk, steamed, 8 oz cup.
- **Iced matcha:** 30 g base, 230 ml milk, 100 g ice, dry shaken, 12 oz cup. Shake hard so the drink stays even.

**Need adjustment**

- **Frappe:** 35 g base, 200 ml milk, 150 g ice, blended 30 seconds, 14 oz cup. A blended drink starts separating as soon as it leaves the blender. Keep it for short delivery radiuses or offer a milkshake instead.
- **Milkshake:** 30 g base, 200 ml milk, 100 g ice, blended 30 seconds, 12 oz cup. Less ice than a frappe, so it holds texture longer.
- **Cordial drinks:** 15 g base, 150 ml sparkling water, 150 g ice. Fizz fades in transit, so consider an iced tea version of the same flavour for delivery.

**Hot coffee drinks** such as RAF (20 g base, 200 ml milk, 20 ml espresso) travel fine in a sealed cup. Our [Raf](/raf-coffee) and [Cream Latte](/cream-latte) bases contain no coffee, so the barista adds fresh espresso at the moment of the order, which keeps quality the same for dine-in and delivery.

## How should you build drinks for delivery?

1. **Use the same dose as dine-in.** Weigh the base every time. A delivery drink that is weaker than the one served at the counter gets noticed by regulars.
2. **Leave 10 to 20 ml headroom.** Overfilled cups leak under the lid. This rule is in our barista standard for every drink, and it matters most in a bag on a scooter.
3. **Shake or stir fully.** Undissolved powder settles during the ride. Shaken and dry-shaken drinks hold an even taste longer.
4. **Keep accents to two.** The flavour rule of maximum two accents per drink also keeps delivery builds simple and quick at peak hours.
5. **Seal and label.** Mark the drink name, size and any changes such as plant milk on the lid.

## How do you pack toppings, foam and garnish?

These are the parts that fail first.

- **Cold foam and cream crowns:** standard [topping](/topping) is 50 g with 250 ml milk, ratio 1:5. A foam crown can sink into the drink over a long route. For delivery, use a domed lid or offer the drink without the crown.
- **Sauces:** chocolate and caramel sauce at 15 to 20 g are best pumped inside the cup or into the pitcher, not drizzled on the rim where they smear on the lid.
- **Freeze dried garnish:** 1 to 2 g. Pack it in a small separate pot so it stays crisp and the customer adds it on arrival.

## FAQ

### What is the best iced drink for delivery?

Shaken iced tea and iced chai hold up best because they are water or milk based without a blended texture. Frappes and sparkling drinks change the most in transit.

### How do I stop iced drinks from leaking in delivery bags?

Leave 10 to 20 ml headroom under the lid, use sealed lids and keep cups upright in a cup carrier. An overfilled cup leaks as soon as the bag tilts.

### Can powder drink bases be used for delivery drinks?

Yes. Weighed powder bases give the same strength every time, so the delivery drink matches the one served at the counter. Dissolve fully by steaming, shaking or blending before sealing.

### Should cold foam go on delivery drinks?

Only on short routes with a domed lid. On longer trips, offer the drink without the crown or serve the foam in a separate container.
