A café can run a full mocktail menu with two dry bases: a **cordial base** for sparkling mojitos and an **iced tea base** for shaken teas. Each drink takes 15 to 20 g of base, 150 ml of water and 150 g of ice in a 12 oz cup, plus fresh fruit or herbs for the signature touch. Below are five tested builds with exact doses.

## Why are cafés adding mocktails now?

Alcohol free drinks are no longer a bar niche. In the UAE, restaurants and cafés increasingly list [considered non-alcoholic menus](https://www.iccadubai.ae/stockpot/what-are-mocktails) alongside coffee, and US trade press reports that [coffee shops are building mocktail programmes](https://freshcup.com/these-coffee-shops-winning-with-mocktails-heres-how-with-recipes/) priced closer to cocktails than to sodas.

For a GCC café the timing is practical:

- **Terrace season** brings guests outdoors in the evening.
- **Festive season and New Year** create demand for a celebratory drink that is not a latte.
- **Halal menus** need a drink that feels like a cocktail with no alcohol at all.

## What is the standard dose for a cordial mojito and an iced tea?

From THE BASE barista standard card:

| Drink | Base | Liquid | Ice | Cup | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cordial mojito | 15 g | Sparkling water 150 ml | 150 g | 12 oz | Build in glass |
| Iced tea | 20 g | Water 150 ml | 150 g | 12 oz | Shake |

Two rules keep these drinks consistent across shifts:

1. **Never shake sparkling water.** Dissolve the cordial base by stirring, then build over ice.
2. **Maximum two flavour accents per drink.** Base, flavour, finish.

## Mocktail build 1: Mango Passion Fruit Mojito

12 oz, cold, [Cordial line](/cordial).

- Mojito cordial base 15 g
- Sparkling water 150 ml
- Passion fruit 40 g
- Fresh mango 30 g
- Mint leaves 8 pcs
- Ice 150 g

Stir the base into the sparkling water until dissolved. In the serving glass, lightly muddle mango, passion fruit and mint. Add ice, pour the sparkling mix and stir. Garnish with mango cubes, passion fruit, a lemon and lime slice and 2 mint sprigs.

## Mocktail build 2: StrawRazz Mojito

12 oz, cold, Cordial line.

- Mojito cordial base 15 g
- Sparkling water 150 ml
- Strawberries and raspberries 60 g
- Basil 2 g, plus mint leaves
- Ice 150 g

Add base, berries, basil and mint to the glass and muddle gently. Add ice, pour sparkling water and stir. Garnish with a lemon and lime wheel, a mint sprig and a strawberry.

## Mocktail build 3: LyPeach Basil Iced Tea

12 oz, cold, [Iced Tea line](/iced-tea).

- Peach iced tea base 20 g
- Water 150 ml
- Fresh lychee 40 g
- Basil leaves 5 pcs
- Ice 150 g

Add water and base to a shaker. Muddle the lychee and basil, then dry shake. Pour over ice in the serving glass. Top with lychee and a basil sprig.

## Mocktail build 4: Iced Pandan Tea

12 oz, cold, Iced Tea line.

- Green iced tea base 20 g
- Water 150 ml
- Pandan syrup 10 ml
- Ice 150 g

Dry shake water, base and pandan syrup without ice until fully mixed and slightly frothy. Pour over ice. Garnish with one knotted pandan leaf.

## Mocktail build 5: Green Tropic

12 oz, cold, Iced Tea line.

- Green iced tea base 20 g
- Water 150 ml
- Pineapple syrup 30 ml
- Lemongrass syrup 10 ml
- Ice 150 g

Dry shake everything except the ice, then pour over ice. Optional garnish: a lemongrass stalk or pineapple wedge.

## How many mocktails does 1 kg of base make?

Calculated from the standard doses:

- **Cordial base at 15 g:** about 66 drinks per kg.
- **Iced tea base at 20 g:** 50 drinks per kg.

Prep fruit before service and keep herbs chilled so a mocktail takes no longer to build than an iced latte.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a mojito and a mocktail?

A classic mojito contains rum. A café mojito is a mocktail: cordial base, sparkling water, citrus, mint and ice, with no alcohol at all.

### Can I shake a mojito made with sparkling water?

No. Shaking releases the carbonation and the drink goes flat. Stir the cordial base into the sparkling water and build the drink in the glass over ice.

### Can iced tea mocktails be batched before service?

Yes. Dissolve the iced tea base in water at the 20 g to 150 ml ratio, chill, then shake individual portions with fruit or syrup to order.
