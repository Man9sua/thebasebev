To price a café drink, cost it first and price it second. Add up every gram and millilitre in the cup, plus packaging and a waste allowance, then divide that cost per cup by your target beverage cost, which for most specialty cafés sits between 20 and 30%. In the UAE, add 5% VAT on top, because the price on the menu board has to include it.

## What goes into the cost per cup?

Cost per cup is more than the main ingredient. A complete recipe cost has one line for each of these:

- **Base or coffee**, by weighed dose
- **Liquid**: milk, plant milk, water or sparkling water
- **Ice** for cold and blended drinks
- **Packaging**: cup, lid, straw, sleeve
- **Accents**: sauce, purée, cold foam, garnish
- **Waste allowance**: remakes, spills, milk left in the pitcher

[Mahalia Coffee's pricing guide](https://mahaliacoffee.com.au/blogs/trade-wholesale-resources/how-to-price-your-cafes-coffee-menu-for-profit) treats cup, lid and wastage as separate lines: small per cup, but on every cup.

For each line, the calculation is the same: **pack price ÷ pack size × dose**.

## What beverage cost percentage should a café target?

Published benchmarks cluster in a narrow band. Toast puts [ideal beverage cost at 15 to 25% per item](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/coffee-shop-menu-pricing), and Texas Coffee School keeps [cost of goods between 20 and 28% of the menu price](https://texascoffeeschool.com/the-2-biggest-things-killing-coffee-shops/). Espresso sits low, milk drinks with sauces sit higher, and your own rent, wages and profit target set the final number.

## What is the formula for a menu price?

Two steps:

1. **Net price = cost per cup ÷ target beverage cost.** Toast's worked example: an ingredient cost of $0.80 divided by an 18% target (0.18) gives $4.44.
2. **Board price in the UAE = net price × 1.05**, to include 5% VAT.

Then round. Round **up** to a price you can hold. Never round down on a drink that is already below target.

## Do UAE cafés have to show prices including VAT?

Yes. The Federal Tax Authority requires [prices displayed to consumers to include VAT](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/is-vat-already-included-in-uae-prices-or-added-at-checkout-1.500323158), and showing tax exclusive prices carries an administrative penalty. Apply the same VAT inclusive price on delivery apps, and remember that when you run a discount, [VAT is calculated on the discounted value](https://tallysolutions.com/mena/uae-vat/vat-compliance-restaurant-fb-uae/), not the original price.

## Why do fixed doses matter for pricing?

A cost per cup is only as accurate as the dose behind it. These are the standard doses from our barista training card:

- **[Chai latte](/chai-latte)**: 25 g base, 150 ml milk, 8 oz cup
- **[Frappe](/frappe)**: 35 g base, 200 ml milk, 150 g ice, 14 oz cup
- **[Cordial](/cordial)**: 15 g base, 150 ml sparkling water, 150 g ice, 12 oz cup
- **Iced matcha**: 30 g base, 230 ml milk, 100 g ice, 12 oz cup

When baristas scoop by eye, the cost sheet stops matching the cup. A frappe dose that drifts from 35 g to 45 g raises the base cost of that cup by about 29%, and the menu price does not move with it.

## Which drinks are most often underpriced?

Drinks with several components, such as dirty chais and mochas. Cost and price each add on separately:

- An extra espresso shot
- Plant milk instead of dairy
- A cold foam layer (50 g on a 12 oz cold drink in our standard)
- Sauce, purée or garnish

If an add on is free, its cost has to be inside the base price of the drink.

## How should you price different cup sizes?

Check every size against the target on its own. A larger cup does not just add milk. Hot matcha uses 20 g base in an 8 oz cup, while iced matcha uses 30 g base in a 12 oz cup: 50% more base, plus ice and a bigger cup. A size upcharge copied from the hot menu can leave the iced version below target.

## FAQ

### What is a good beverage cost percentage for a café?

Most published benchmarks sit between 15 and 30% of the menu price. Plain coffee runs lower and dessert style milk drinks run higher, so set the target from your own overheads.

### Should VAT be included in the cost per cup?

No. Cost the cup without VAT, calculate the net price, then multiply by 1.05 for the board price. In the UAE the displayed price must include VAT.

### How do I stop the cost per cup from drifting?

Weigh doses, mark fill lines on jugs, assign one cup size per drink and recheck scoops against the scale every month.
