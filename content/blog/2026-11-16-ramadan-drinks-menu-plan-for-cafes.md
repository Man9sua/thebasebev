A café should start building its Ramadan drinks menu about 12 weeks before the first fast, which for Ramadan 2027 means now. Pick four to six drinks, test them by mid December, train staff and stock up in January, and plan for two peaks a day: iftar and suhoor.

## When does Ramadan 2027 start in the UAE?

Ramadan 2027 is [expected to begin on 8 February 2027 in the UAE](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/ramadan-eid-al-fitr-eid-al-adha-2027-likely-dates), based on Astronomy Centre calculations, with Eid Al Fitr around 10 March. The final date depends on moon sighting, so plan a launch window of one or two days.

## When should a café start planning its Ramadan drinks menu?

Work backwards from 8 February:

1. **Mid November to mid December:** shortlist drinks, request samples, run tastings with your team and a few regulars.
2. **Second half of December:** lock recipe cards, cost each cup, shoot menu photos.
3. **January:** train every shift on the new builds, place your first order and build stock.
4. **Last week of January:** final run through at full speed, stock on shelf, menu boards printed.

This leaves room for one round of recipe changes and one supplier delay.

## Which drinks work for iftar and suhoor?

Guests breaking the fast usually want something cold, light and fruity first. Later in the evening and at suhoor, orders shift to warm, spiced milk drinks and blended dessert drinks for groups who stay late. A practical list has one of each: a sparkling fruit drink, a milkshake, a hot spiced drink and a frappe.

## Four Ramadan builds with exact doses

### Lychee Petal Mojito (iftar, 12 oz, no alcohol)

1. Dissolve 15 g Red Rose Lemon Cordial Base in 150 ml sparkling water.
2. Lightly muddle 30 g fresh lychee and 6 mint leaves in the glass.
3. Add 150 g ice (cubed or crushed) and pour.
4. Garnish with 5 g dried rose petals, 2 mint sprigs, a lemon slice and a lime slice.

See the [cordial range](/cordial) for other flavours on the same build.

### Peach Rose Milkshake (iftar, 12 oz)

1. Blend 30 g Yogurt Base, 200 ml milk, 60 g frozen peach slices, 20 ml rose water and 100 g ice until smooth.
2. Pour into a 12 oz glass, garnish with peach slices and rose petals.

The [milkshake bases](/milkshake) follow the same 30 g base plus 200 ml milk logic, so staff learn one ratio.

### Nutty Masala (suhoor, hot, 8 oz)

1. In a pitcher, combine 25 g Masala Chai base, 200 ml milk and 10 g cashew paste.
2. Steam to 65°C until frothy and smooth.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup and finish with 5 g cashew crumbs.

More spiced options are on the [chai latte page](/chai-latte).

### Rose Pistachio Frappe (late evening, 12 oz)

1. Blend 35 g Pistachio Frappe Base, 200 ml milk, 30 ml espresso, 10 ml rose syrup, 10 g white chocolate sauce and ice until smooth.
2. Pour into a 12 oz cup.
3. Top with 30 g cream cheese topping, 2 g crushed pistachio and a pinch of dried rose petals.

## How do you keep service fast at the iftar peak?

Most orders land in a short window around sunset, so speed comes from preparation.

1. **Portion dry bases in advance.** Weigh each powder dose into lidded cups before the peak.
2. **Prep fresh garnish once.** Wash mint, cut citrus wheels and portion fruit into labelled containers before sunset.
3. **Split the bar.** One station for cold builds (shaker and blender), one for steamed drinks.
4. **Keep the Ramadan list short.** Four to six specials are enough.
5. **Offer plant milk.** Milk based builds can be made with plant milk.

## How much stock should you order for the month?

Per drink: cups per day × base dose × days, plus a buffer.

Example: 40 Nutty Masala a night is 40 × 25 g = 1 kg of base a day, about 30 kg for the month. Order half, track a week, then top up. With no minimum order and a lead time from 15 days, a reorder placed in the first week of Ramadan arrives in time.
