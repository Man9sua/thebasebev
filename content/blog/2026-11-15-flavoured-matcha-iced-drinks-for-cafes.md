Flavoured matcha drinks are iced or hot matcha lattes built on a dry matcha base that already carries a fruit or dessert flavour, such as raspberry, strawberry or blueberry. A café can run a full signature matcha menu from one standard: 25 g base, 230 ml milk and 100 g ice in a 12 oz cup, dry shaken. Below are four builds with exact doses.

## Why are cafés adding flavoured matcha to the menu?

Matcha demand keeps growing while supply stays tight. [Perfect Daily Grind reported](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2026/08/matcha-is-everywhere-japan/) that first flush tencha prices in Kyoto rose from JPY 5,500/kg in 2024 to JPY 14,333/kg in 2025, and that some foodservice buyers are now looking beyond pure Japanese matcha.

For a café in the UAE or GCC, flavoured matcha bases solve three practical problems:

- **One ingredient, one weigh.** Flavour, sweetness and matcha come in one powder, so the barista weighs a single dose instead of whisking, sweetening and flavouring separately.
- **Colour sells the drink.** Pink, red and blue matcha drinks photograph well without extra garnish.
- **Menu depth from one routine.** Every build below uses the same cup, milk and ice standard.

## What is the standard for an iced flavoured matcha?

All four builds share this frame:

1. Cup: **12 oz**.
2. Base: **25 g** flavoured matcha base.
3. Milk: **230 ml**.
4. Ice: **100 g** in the cup.
5. Method: **dry shake** the base, milk and syrup until fully mixed and slightly frothy, then pour over ice.

Dry shaking (shaking without ice) dissolves the powder fully and builds a light foam. Add the ice in the cup, not the shaker, so the drink is not diluted before it reaches the guest.

## How do you make a Basilberry Matcha?

Raspberry matcha with a fresh herbal finish.

- 25 g raspberry [matcha base](/matcha)
- 230 ml milk
- 10 ml basil syrup
- 100 g ice

1. Add the milk, raspberry matcha base and basil syrup to a cocktail shaker.
2. Dry shake well.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with a basil leaf, dried raspberry or a dusting of raspberry [garnish](/garnish).

## How do you make a Sakura Bloom?

A soft pink drink with pear and thyme, light enough for all day service.

- 25 g pink matcha base
- 230 ml milk
- 20 g pear purée
- 5 ml thyme syrup
- 100 g ice

1. Add the milk, pink matcha base, pear purée and thyme syrup to a shaker.
2. Dry shake well.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Garnish with a thyme sprig or a pear slice.

## How do you make a Pistachio Berry Matcha?

Strawberry matcha with pistachio, a pairing Gulf guests already know from desserts.

- 25 g strawberry matcha base
- 230 ml milk
- 15 ml pistachio syrup
- 100 g ice
- 5 g crushed pistachios

1. Add the milk, strawberry matcha base and pistachio syrup to a shaker.
2. Dry shake well.
3. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
4. Top with 5 g crushed pistachios.

## How do you make a Black Anchan?

Blue matcha under a dark, salty sweet sesame layer. The most visual drink of the four.

- 25 g anchan blueberry base
- 230 ml milk
- 100 g ice
- 10 g black sesame paste
- 15 ml vanilla syrup
- A pinch of salt

1. Dry shake the milk and anchan blueberry base until smooth.
2. Pour into a 12 oz cup over ice.
3. Mix the black sesame paste, vanilla syrup and salt, then spoon it on top.
4. Garnish with crushed sesame seeds or dried butterfly pea flowers.

## Can you still offer pure matcha alongside flavoured bases?

Yes, and many cafés keep both. Pure powder is for guests who order a plain matcha latte. Our standard for pure powders in an iced 12 oz drink is 4 g whisked in 40 ml water at 70°C, then 210 ml milk and 80 g ice. Flavoured bases cover the signature and seasonal side of the menu.

## FAQ

### How much flavoured matcha base goes into an iced latte?

The builds above use 25 g of base with 230 ml milk and 100 g ice in a 12 oz cup. The syrups and purées add flavour accents without changing the base dose.

### Can these drinks be made with oat or almond milk?

Yes. The bases work with plant milk, so keep the same 230 ml and dry shake as normal.

### Can flavoured matcha be served hot?

Yes. For a hot 8 oz drink, steam 20 g of matcha base with 200 ml milk to 65°C.

### Why dry shake instead of shaking with ice?

Dry shaking dissolves the powder fully and builds a light foam without diluting the drink. The ice goes into the cup afterwards.
