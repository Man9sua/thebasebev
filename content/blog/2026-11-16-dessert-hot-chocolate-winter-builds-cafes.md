A dessert hot chocolate is a chocolate drink built to taste like a familiar dessert: orange truffle, s'mores, pistachio or banana bread. For a café, the simplest way to run one is a flavoured chocolate base steamed with milk, so each cup needs one scoop, one pitcher and under a minute at the bar. Below are four winter builds with exact doses for an 8 oz cup.

## Why are dessert flavoured hot drinks on winter menus?

Winter menus now change through small seasonal additions rather than full rewrites. Trend trackers point to [dessert themed café drinks](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/third-wave-coffee-happy-holidays-menu) that borrow flavours from pastries and sweets, and to [pistachio replacing bitter profiles](https://coffeegeography.com/2026/01/10/coffee-culture-evolves-winter-menus-swap-bitterness-for-protein-and-pistachio-to-captivate-next-gen-drinkers/) on winter boards. In the GCC this fits a customer base that already orders chocolate and pistachio desserts, and many guests who skip coffee.

For the operator, the logic is simple:

1. One chocolate line covers several seasonal drinks.
2. No syrups to batch, no sauces to temper.
3. Recipes are short enough to train a new barista in one shift.

## What cup size and milk volume should you standardise?

All four builds below use the same frame:

1. **Cup:** 8 oz (237 ml), target fill 227 ml, 10 ml headroom.
2. **Milk:** start at 180 ml and adjust so the final drink keeps 10 ml headroom after steaming expansion and foam.
3. **Temperature:** steam base and milk together to 60 to 65°C.

Keeping one frame for the whole winter list means the same pitcher, the same steaming time and one recipe card on the wall.

## How do you make an orange chocolate truffle latte?

1. Add 35 g Chocolate Orange BASE and 180 ml milk to a steaming pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 60 to 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.
4. Finish with a light dusting of ground cinnamon.

Orange and chocolate is a classic pairing that reads well on a winter board and needs no garnish beyond cinnamon.

## How do you make a strawberry chocolate s'mores latte?

1. Add 35 g Chocolate Strawberry BASE and 180 ml milk to a steaming pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 60 to 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.
4. Arrange 5 to 8 g mini marshmallows on top.
5. Toast lightly with a kitchen torch until golden brown.

Safety point for the bar: toast before handing over, and keep the flame away from the cup, sleeves and anything flammable.

## How do you make a pistachio mocha?

1. Add 35 g Chocolate Mocha BASE, 5 g Sugar Free Pistachio BASE and milk to a pitcher.
2. Steam until smooth and 60 to 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving 10 ml headroom.

The recipe sheet lists 200 ml milk for this build. With 40 g of base in an 8 oz cup, check the fill on your first cups and reduce the milk if needed to keep the 10 ml headroom.

## Is there a dessert drink without chocolate?

Yes. A banana bread hojicha gives the same dessert profile with roasted tea instead of cocoa:

1. Combine 5 g Hojicha Powder, 10 g Banana Ice Cream Salted Caramel RAF base and 180 ml milk.
2. Steam until smooth and 65°C.
3. Pour into an 8 oz cup, leaving room for foam.
4. Top with 20 g Banana Bread Cold Foam.

It gives non chocolate drinkers a seasonal option and uses the same steaming routine as the rest of the list.

## FAQ

### What temperature should hot chocolate be steamed to?

These builds use 60 to 65°C. That range keeps the milk sweet and the chocolate smooth without scalding.

### Can dessert hot chocolates be made with oat or almond milk?

Yes. THE BASE bases work with plant milk, so the same doses apply. Check the fill on the first cups, since plant milks foam differently.

### How many winter dessert drinks should a café add?

Start with two or three that share one base line, such as orange and strawberry chocolate. That adds variety without extra stock or new prep steps.

### Can these drinks be served iced?

Yes, the bases can be served iced, which helps on warm GCC winter afternoons. Ask the team for iced doses when you request samples.
